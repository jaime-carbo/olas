This application is running inside one of the technologies I like the most, Kubernetes. I've worked with it for a couple of years now and, even though it might be a bit overkill, I decided to build this website inside a cluster that lives in a rented server. Here you can see the resources that it is consuming and the time the pod that is serving you has been living for (which will probably be the time since the last push to this <a href="https://github.com/jaime-carbo/olas">Github Repo</a>).
